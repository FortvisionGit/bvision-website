Information about us

BVision.AI (operated by FORTVISION LTD) acts as both a Controller (for our own data collection) and a Processor (for data we process on behalf of our clients).

BVision.AI is the brand name and platform of FORTVISION LTD, a company incorporated under the laws of Israel with company registration number 515224905, and whose registered address is in Israel ("FORTVISION", "BVision.AI", "we", "us", or "our").

For the purposes of the European Union's ("EU") General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR") and other applicable privacy laws, BVision.AI is a data controller ("Controller") in relation to the personal data of our former and current employees, directors, officers, management, representatives, clients, service providers, website visitors and whistleblowers. Likewise, BVision.AI is a Business (as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (together, "CCPA")), and other US state privacy laws, subject to their applicability to BVision.AI.

For the purposes of the GDPR and other applicable privacy laws, BVision.AI is a data processor ("Processor") in relation to your personal data processed in connection with our provision of the Services. Therefore, when processing such personal data, we are doing so on behalf of our clients, who are Controllers of such personal data, and such processing is governed by the applicable Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") with the relevant Controller (i.e., client).

If your personal data is provided to us by a client in connection with the provision of the Services, please contact our relevant client in order to receive further information regarding the processing of your personal data in connection with the Services.

As FORTVISION LTD is incorporated in Israel — a country recognized by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection — transfers of Personal Data from the EEA to BVision.AI's primary operations in Israel benefit from an EU adequacy ruling.

Information we collect and how we collect it

We collect various categories of personal data in order to meet our contractual and legal obligations and legitimate interests, such as fraud prevention.

2.1 Engagements with Employees

We obtain your Personal Data from you directly, for example, during the course of your employment. Personal Data we collect includes: your name, social security or identification number, mailing address, email address, phone number, age, gender, salary, education, professional designation, employment history, resume details, references, interview notes, performance review notes, and emergency contact information.

We collect this data for: fulfillment of our contractual obligations to you (Performance of a Contract); evaluation of your candidacy for employment (Legitimate Interests); general business management (Legitimate Interests); and statutory reporting obligations (Legal Obligation).

2.2 Engagements with Clients and Service Providers

We may obtain your Personal Data from you directly or publicly-available sources. Personal Data we collect includes: your business name, name of your representative, business mailing address, business email address, business phone number, business payment details, and job title of your representative. For anti-money laundering requirements, we may also collect date of birth, utility bill information, copies of passports or identification cards, and nationality.

We collect this data for: fulfillment of our contractual obligations (Performance of a Contract); establishment and management of our relationship (Legitimate Interests); general business management (Legitimate Interests); security management (Legitimate Interests and Legal Obligation); and statutory reporting obligations (Legal Obligation).

2.3 Processing of Personal Data in Connection with the Services

We may obtain your Personal Data from you directly or from our clients. Personal Data we collect includes: your name, email address, username, password, audio recordings of support service calls, and the phone number of the caller (excluding identification numbers and credit card numbers).

Please note that any Personal Data processed as described in this section is processed by us in our role as a Processor on behalf of our clients in their role as a Controller, in connection with our provision of Services to them.

2.4 Whistleblowing

If you submit a whistleblower report through our internal reporting channel, we collect your name, email address, and employee number. You may submit anonymously; if you do, we collect only information included in the report itself. We process this data to comply with whistleblowing standards and legal requirements (Legitimate Interests; Legal Obligation).

2.5 Website Visitors

When you access our website, we or our third-party service providers may collect: your IP address, URL, type of operating system, browser type, browser plug-in versions, screen resolution, Flash version, time zone setting, clickstream data, the period of time you accessed our website, and methods used to browse away from our website. If you object to our use of your Personal Data for any of the aforementioned purposes, please contact us at privacy@bvision.ai.

International data transfers

We transfer Personal Data internationally with appropriate safeguards in place, including EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

For more information about these safeguards, please contact us at privacy@bvision.ai.

7

Data collection from our website and from cookies

We use only essential (functional) cookies. We do not use marketing, advertising, or non-essential tracking cookies.

Our website uses cookies, pixel tags and other forms of identification and local storage (collectively, "cookies") to distinguish you from other visitors of our website. This helps us to provide you with an optimal user experience and allows us to provide and improve our website.

Cookies are computer files containing small amounts of information which are downloaded to a user's device when visiting a website. Cookies fall into several distinct categories:

  • Essential cookies: Strictly necessary for the website to function, such as enabling page navigation, access to secure areas, and core functionalities
  • Functional cookies: Enhance usability and performance by remembering visitor preferences and settings
  • Analytics cookies: Used to carry out website performance and audience metrics
  • Marketing cookies: Used for tracking visitor behavior across websites for targeted advertising

BVision.AI uses only essential (functional) cookies. We do not use non-essential cookies such as cookies for marketing and advertising purposes. Functionality cookies do not require your consent.

You can block cookies by activating the settings on your browser that allow you to refuse the settings of all or some cookies. However, if you block all cookies, you may not be able to access all or parts of our website. For more information on managing cookies, visit aboutcookies.org.

8

Security and storage of data

We take data security very seriously, invest in security systems, and train our staff. In the event of a breach, we make the appropriate notifications as required by law.

We take great care in implementing, enforcing and maintaining the security of the Personal Data we process. BVision.AI implements, enforces and maintains security measures, technologies and policies to prevent the unauthorized or accidental access to or destruction, loss, modification, use or disclosure of Personal Data. Our security practices include:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest; TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege access principles
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all internal systems
  • Regular penetration testing by independent third parties
  • SOC 2 Type II certification, reviewed annually
  • Continuous security monitoring and anomaly detection
  • Periodic security awareness training for all employees

Please note that no data security measures are perfect or impenetrable and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, leaks, viruses and other data security breaches will never occur.

We endeavor to limit access to Personal Data to those of our personnel who require access to fulfill our obligations, have been appropriately trained with respect to the requirements applicable to the processing of Personal Data, and are under confidentiality obligations as required under applicable law.

We act in accordance with applicable law to promptly notify the relevant authorities and Data Subjects in the event that any Personal Data is lost, stolen, modified or disclosed or where there has been unauthorized access to it. BVision.AI shall promptly take reasonable remedial measures.

Data subject rights

Depending on the law applicable to your Personal Data, you may have various rights. We will respect any lawful request to exercise such rights.

Data Subjects in certain jurisdictions, such as in the EU and the UK, have rights granted pursuant to local laws under certain circumstances and with certain exceptions, including:

Rectification

Erasure
Have your Personal Data held by us deleted, subject to legal exceptions.

Objection

To exercise any of your rights, contact us at privacy@bvision.ai. 11

Do Not Track signals

Do Not Track ("DNT") is a privacy preference that users can set in certain web browsers to inform websites that they do not wish to be tracked. BVision.AI honors DNT signals. When a valid DNT signal is detected, we do not place non-essential tracking technologies on your device.
